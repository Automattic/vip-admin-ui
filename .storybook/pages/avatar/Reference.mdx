## Style

### Interactive states

The avatar has no pointer or keyboard treatment.

### Typography

Initials use the `heading-sm` text style.<DevNote><code>font: var(--vipui-text-heading-sm)</code></DevNote>

### Structure

The box has a fixed size and does not shrink beside a long name. Its radius and initials type come from the package tokens. <Convention name="setup" />
