### Before you ship

The component does not supply these. Specify them in the design:

- Show the actor's name as text beside every avatar. The avatar has no accessible name of its own.
- Choose the icon for each kind of actor that is not a person, and use it everywhere that actor appears.

The table does not show the following:

- `name` is required. It is the source of the initials.
- `size` accepts `sm` and `2xs` only.
- `src` and `icon` are optional. `icon` replaces the initials, and a picture that loads replaces both.
