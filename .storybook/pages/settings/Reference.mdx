## Style

### Interactive states

The section, the save bar and the loading row have no pointer or keyboard treatment. Controls inside a section keep their own states. <Convention name="focus-ring" />

## Accessibility

### Labeling and states

A section is announced as a group named by its title. Its description is read with the group's name. <DevNote>`Fieldset.Root` sets `aria-labelledby` to the legend and `aria-describedby` to `Fieldset.Description`.</DevNote>

The loading row's words come from the plugin. <Convention name="strings" />
