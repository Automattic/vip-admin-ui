## States

A modal is open, saving, showing an error, or blocking. See the Build page for each one.

A confirmation resolves to yes when the user picks the confirm button, and to no when they cancel, close or dismiss it. A confirmation still waiting when a new one opens, or when the page leaves, resolves to no. <DevNote>`confirm()` returns a promise of `true` or `false`. A pending one settles `false` on a new `confirm()` call or on unmount.</DevNote>

## Style

### Interactive states

The body and footer have no pointer or keyboard treatment. The buttons and the close button have the states of the WPDS `Button`. <Convention name="focus-ring" />

The footer has a larger gap above it than between the body's blocks. <DevNote>`margin-top: var(--wpds-dimension-gap-xl)` on `.vipui-modal-actions`. A `Modal` body is not a `Stack`, so the footer sets its own gap.</DevNote>

### Labeling and states

- The modal is announced as a dialog named by its title. <DevNote>The WPDS `Modal` sets `role="dialog"` and labels it with the title heading.</DevNote>
- A saving button is announced as busy and disabled. <DevNote>`isBusy` and `disabled` on the WPDS `Button`.</DevNote>
- A confirmation's buttons use the words from the plugin's strings when no labels are given. <Convention name="strings" />
