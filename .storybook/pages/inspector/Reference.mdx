## Style

### Interactive states

A row that opens something is underlined on hover. A collapsible section's title and chevron change tone on hover. A field list's grips and remove buttons appear on hover, or when the row has focus. <Convention name="focus-ring" also="Rows, grips and section toggles use the WPDS focus-ring style." />

### Labeling and states

- The collapse toggle is named "Collapse panel" or "Expand panel", and reports whether the panel is open. <DevNote>`aria-expanded`. The words come from `StringsProvider` (`collapsePanel`, `expandPanel`).</DevNote>
- A row that opens something is a button named for its item. A grip has its own name, different from the row's. <DevNote>`selectLabel` on `Fact`. In a field list, `configureItem`, `selectItem` and `reorderItem` from `StringsProvider`.</DevNote>
- An info tip is named for the setting it explains. <DevNote>`aboutSetting( about )` from `StringsProvider`.</DevNote>
- A closed section's controls are still found by find-in-page, which opens the section. <DevNote>`hiddenUntilFound` on the collapsible panel.</DevNote>
- An unavailable delete button stays in the tab order, and its reason is announced. <DevNote>`accessibleWhenDisabled` on the `Button`, and `aria-describedby` to the `description`.</DevNote>
