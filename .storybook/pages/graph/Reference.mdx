### Interactions

**Mouse:** Click a card, an edge's pill or a band label to select it. Drag a card to move it. Drag from a card's grip or an exit badge to another card to connect them. Drag a selected edge's ring to move that end. Right-click a card, an edge, a band label or empty canvas to open its menu.

**Keyboard:** See Keyboard interactions.

## Style

### Interactive states

Hovering a card darkens its border and shows its grip or exit badges. Hovering an edge shows its pill. A selected card, edge or band takes the brand colour. <Convention name="focus-ring" also="Cards, edges and menu items use the WPDS focus stroke." />

### Structure

A card is 200 by 80 pixels and a Start or End pill is 120 by 36. Your layout positions them.

### Labeling and states

- A card's warnings are announced as the name of its flag. <DevNote><code>data.warnings</code></DevNote>
- A card can have screen-reader text for state the canvas shows another way. <DevNote><code>data.description</code></DevNote>
- The link mark on an edge drawn more than once has its own accessible name. <DevNote><code>data.linkLabel</code></DevNote>
- A menu is named for what it acts on, and its items are menu items. <DevNote>The <code>label</code> returned from <code>contextMenu</code>. The menu is <code>role="menu"</code> with one tab stop.</DevNote>
